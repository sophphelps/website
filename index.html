About Me

I'm a passionate astrophysics student at UCLA with a deep fascination for the cosmos. Currently working as a NASA JPL intern in the Exoplanet Astrophysics Lab, I'm dedicated to advancing our understanding of stellar magnetic activity and its impact on radial velocity precision.

My research focuses on analyzing high-resolution, disk-integrated solar spectra in the near-infrared using SoCal, examining Zeeman-sensitive Fe I lines as magnetic activity indicators. Through this work, I hope to contribute to the detection and characterization of exoplanets around other stars.

Beyond research, I'm passionate about education and mentorship. As a co-founder of StudySTEM, I've tutored over 100 students and generated $100k+ in revenue while helping others discover the beauty of mathematics and physics.

Research Interests

  • Exoplanet detection and characterization
  • Stellar magnetic activity
  • Radial velocity precision
  • Near-infrared spectroscopy
  • Telluric modeling and correction

Near-Infrared Solar Line Diagnostics of Magnetic Activity
